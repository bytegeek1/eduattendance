Workflow

Attendance modes

One system, three registers

Schools do not all take attendance the same way — and the same school rarely does it one way throughout. Set the mode for the school, then override it for any section that works differently.

daily

Once a day

The class teacher takes one roll call in the morning. That mark stands for the whole day.

  • No timetable required
  • One register per section per day
  • Reports count days, not periods

Best for primary classes and small schools.

period

Every period

The timetable opens a register for each period. Subject teachers mark their own.

  • Needs a timetable per class
  • Subject-wise attendance rates
  • Catches period-skipping

Best for secondary and senior classes.

checkinout

Gate / check in–out

A reader records each arrival — and each departure, if you require check-out. Teachers mark nothing: late, half-day and hours inside are worked out from the punches.

  • Needs a reader at the gate or door
  • Exact in and out times kept
  • Turn Require check-out off for an arrival-only gate

Best with a biometric or card reader.

The menu follows the mode. Screens a mode has no use for simply disappear — but if one section still needs the timetable, the timetable stays. And whichever mode a section runs, the day is stored the same way, so every report and every parent screen reads identically.

Capabilities

Everything the office stops doing by hand

Not a feature list written to look long — this is what the software does on day one.

CSV student import

Headings are matched loosely, so a spreadsheet from any source works. Every row is validated and previewed before a single record is written.

Classes, sections & subjects

Build the school once — classes, their sections, the subjects each class takes — and the registers, timetable and reports follow from it.

Timetable & periods

Define period times once, then lay out the week per section. Period-wise registers open automatically against the right subject and teacher.

Leave applications

Parents apply from their own screen, teachers and the office approve, and an approved leave stops the absence message going out.

Holidays & academic years

Mark the calendar and a holiday is never counted as an absence — no false alerts to parents on a day the school was shut.

Promotion & transfers

Move a whole class up at year end, or shift one child between sections, without ever losing the record behind them.

Attendance certificates

A clean, printable certificate for any student over any date range — on the school's own letterhead, ready to sign and stamp.

Audited settings & sign-in

Every settings change is recorded against the person who made it, and repeated failed sign-ins are throttled at the door.

Scheduled backups

The database is dumped on a schedule and kept where you choose, so a bad day is an inconvenience rather than a catastrophe.
